# Qismat Competitive Product Strategy

Last reviewed: 2026-09-26

## Positioning

Qismat is a privacy-first, human-moderated matrimonial platform for adults from every community who are genuinely seeking marriage. It should combine the structured search expected from established matrimonial services with the safety, clarity and member control expected from modern relationship products.

Qismat will differentiate through:

- marriage-only participation and explicit truthful-profile commitments;
- one server-enforced privacy and eligibility model across web, Android and iOS;
- human profile and photo moderation with clear member feedback;
- explainable compatibility instead of an opaque universal score;
- optional, member-controlled family or trusted-person participation;
- private photos and communication that open gradually with mutual consent;
- safety controls that remain available throughout discovery, interests, messaging and offline meetings.

## Market benchmark

The active benchmark set includes Shaadi.com, BharatMatrimony, Jeevansathi, Muzz, Salams, Dil Mil, Betterhalf, Hinge, Bumble and eHarmony. Their strongest relevant patterns are identity and photo verification, granular privacy, advanced discovery, mutual communication, family or chaperone support, compatibility prompts, respectful-message intervention, trusted-contact meeting plans, subscriptions and assisted matchmaking.

## Approved delivery priorities

### Launch integrity

1. Complete sent and received interests, acceptance, decline, cancellation and mutual connection state.
2. Add bidirectional blocking, confidential member reports and an audited administrator safety queue.
3. Add mutual-connection messaging with unread/read state, rate limits and report-from-conversation.
4. Add pause, hide, session revocation, account deletion and public policy/support pages.
5. Add notification preferences, service email and privacy-safe Android push delivery.
6. Use verified email and Google sign-in with clearly defined trust indicators.

### Trust differentiation

1. Add profile prompts and allow an interest to reference a prompt or photo.
2. Add member-controlled photo reveal requests.
3. Add an optional trusted-person/family mode without transferring account ownership.
4. Expand compatibility questions around values, marriage timeline, family expectations, relocation, children, finances, faith and lifestyle.
5. Provide curated recommendations with plain-language compatibility factors.
6. Add trusted-contact meeting plans and post-connection safety feedback.

### Deferred until the safe core is stable

- biometric or government-ID verification without an approved provider and retention policy;
- in-app voice/video calls;
- AI-generated messages;
- paid visibility, boosts and subscriptions;
- assisted matchmaking and community chat rooms;
- horoscope matching without demonstrated audience demand.

## Product guardrails

- Safety controls must never require payment.
- A verification badge must identify exactly what was verified.
- Blocking must immediately remove discovery and communication access in both directions.
- Reports remain confidential and every administrator outcome requires notes and an audit record.
- Family participation is opt-in, revocable and controlled by the member.
- Matching explanations use member-provided factors and never imply certainty about relationship success.
